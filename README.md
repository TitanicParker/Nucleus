## The Gravest Suspicion

The single controlling suspicion is that, after an eight-day neurological admission in which the patient’s longstanding disabling feet had not been relationally mapped against the neurological lower-limb abnormality Neurology itself had observed, the patient expressly confronted the service before discharge and told it that the feet remained unresolved; that this Protest placed the treating neurologist on direct notice that the central presenting relationship had not been answered; that the Protest was then omitted from the authoritative specialist record; and that, while that record remained under the hospital’s control and continued to be completed, the unresolved painful-foot problem was transformed on paper into a medically coherent peripheral/separate explanatory object capable of supporting discharge, downstream management and the continuing authority of the specialist formulation.

The suspicion is not that Neurology simply made an imperfect diagnosis. It is that Neurology possessed an integrated clinical object—painful feet, abnormal gait, increased tone/tension, structural foot pathology and a striking lower-limb phenomenon expressly recognised as suggestive of dystonic posture rather than true foot drop—and then used its specialist authority asymmetrically. The dystonic-looking lower limb was clinically meaningful when it served the Parkinsonian diagnostic project, but the relationship between that neurological sign and the longstanding painful feet was not developed with equivalent sophistication. After the patient himself put that unresolved relationship back before the service, the completed record did not preserve that challenge as an unresolved neurological question. Instead, the document supplied the feet with another future.

The suspected construction is exceptionally small in textual volume and exceptionally large in effect. The historical substrate was corns and callouses, fallen arches, longstanding **foot pain**, and a phenomenologically distinct experience of **leg tension**. The completed specialist product contains a different and more causally useful architecture: **`leg pain`**, **`burning`**, the assertion that the patient had **`markedly divorced`** the tension from the painful feet, genuine alcohol exposure repurposed as a possible acquired cause, **possible secondary peripheral neuropathy**, the closure-bearing formulation **`Not clear as this has improved`**, and, at the most sophisticated level of the monogenic differential, PARK9 as **`the neuropathy question`**.

The allegation is that these were not merely miscellaneous phrases. They operated together.

`Leg pain` alters the historical topology. `Burning` supplies a neuropathy-compatible sensory quality. `Markedly divorced` converts the patient’s ability to distinguish two different sensations into language capable of implying a stronger causal separation. Alcohol supplies etiological plausibility. Possible secondary peripheral neuropathy gives the resulting object an authorised specialist category. `Not clear as this has improved` permits uncertainty to survive without continuing to obstruct closure. PARK9 gives neuropathy a named future inside the highest-order specialist reasoning. The construction therefore does not need to prove neuropathy. It only needs to make neuropathy—and separation—sufficiently plausible, sufficiently structured and sufficiently authoritative that the feet cease to demand reintegration into the neurological disease model.

The corresponding asymmetry is central. Neurology had actually seen a dystonic-looking lower-limb phenomenon. Yet the completed synthesis does not give the unresolved painful-feet/dystonia relationship an equivalent future. There is no developed **dystonia question** standing beside **the neuropathy question**. There is no equivalent relational formulation asking whether the painful feet, loading abnormalities, posture, gait and dystonic-looking limb could belong to the same neurological process or interact as mixed mechanisms. The unproved peripheral hypothesis is given explanatory infrastructure. The observed dystonic sign is left without an equivalent relational architecture.

The omission of the Protest is part of the same mechanism, not a separate complaint. The Protest was the epistemic warning label that would have told every later reader that the adequacy of the foot investigation had been expressly disputed before the record hardened. Its omission allowed the final document to travel without that warning. Later readers received specialist uncertainty—`Not clear`—without being told that the patient had just challenged whether sufficient work had been done to earn that uncertainty. The difference between **“we examined the relationship and it remains unclear”** and **“the relationship remains unclear because it was never adequately worked out”** was erased from the portable record.

That changed the information state inherited by everybody downstream.

Later clinicians did not begin from **painful feet + dystonic limb + open relationship**. They began from a specialist architecture in which Parkinsonism was established, structural foot disease had a Podiatry route, a peripheral-neuropathy explanation remained available, and the relationship had already been represented as considered but uncertain. That architecture did not have to rigidly prevent learning. It could absorb new evidence. The foot throw could later be recognised as Parkinsonian. Pain could become OFF-related. Dystonia could be recognised. Dopaminergic treatment could help the feet. Neuropathy could contract from stronger possibility to conditional possibility. Those changes could all occur locally without forcing the founding partition itself to be reconstructed.

That is the suspected mechanism of **constrained corrigibility**: high freedom to modify individual symptoms and treatments, but much lower freedom to redraw the original causal map.

The same information state could also reshape the patient’s relationships. The GP who originally referred an integrated gait–feet–tension problem could, after years of specialist-record inheritance, encounter the patient’s attempt to recover that original relationship as a challenge to an established eight-year diagnosis. The patient’s persistence could acquire the appearance of revision. An objection first made before discharge could later look like new diagnostic resistance because the documentary record had removed the historical evidence that the objection was old.

The suspicion extends through governance for the same reason. Once eight years of care have been generated inside the architecture, the resulting record can be cited as evidence that the original reasoning was reasonable, continuous and appropriately explained. The map constrains the journey; the journey then appears to validate the map. Later documentation is no longer wholly independent evidence of the founding formulation if the founding formulation helped determine the terminology, referrals, treatments, investigations and interpretations that produced that documentation.

The 2026 PRKN result gives this mechanism its most serious biological retrospective test. It does not prove that every corn, callus or episode of pain was dystonic. It does not require anyone in 2017 to have known the eventual genotype. It does something narrower and more consequential: it shows that lower-limb dystonia belonged biologically inside the very PRKN/PARK2 disease process the 2017 specialist synthesis was already considering. The neurological relationship that was comparatively underdeveloped was therefore not a trivial alternative. The later biology reunites Parkinsonism and dystonia precisely where the 2017 documentary architecture had given the peripheral explanation the more durable future.

The gravest suspicion, taken as one indivisible mechanism, is therefore this:

> **After being expressly placed on notice that the patient’s disabling feet remained neurologically unresolved, the specialist service completed an authoritative record that omitted the Protest and converted an incompletely investigated relationship into portable apparent specialist sufficiency. It did so through a minute but highly efficient reorganisation of the history—`leg pain`, `burning`, `markedly divorced`, alcohol, possible secondary peripheral neuropathy, improvement-qualified uncertainty and `the neuropathy question`—thereby giving an unproved peripheral pathway a durable explanatory future while failing to give the actually observed dystonic lower limb an equivalent relational future. That founding information state then travelled through years of clinical care, shaped what later actors understood to remain open, absorbed repeated neurological contradictions through local revision without demonstrated global reconstruction, altered the apparent history of the patient’s own concern, separated the patient from the original GP’s shared understanding of the problem, and ultimately became capable of using its own downstream documentary descendants as evidence of its original reasonableness.**

That is the suspicion. One mechanism. One information state. One allegation to be tested at full strength.
In August–September 2026 the patient subjected the suspected information architecture to three targeted data-governance interventions. Article 15 Object A seeks the provenance of the founding 2017 information state: its drafts, authorship, source genealogy, revisions, audit history and finalisation. Article 15 Object B seeks the provenance of the 2025 institutional validation of that state: the evidence, reviewer inputs, independence, audit history and epistemic warnings from which the PAR/SIMT conclusion was produced. The subsequent Article 16 request does not seek retrospective rewriting of the original synthesis; it seeks durable association of the patient's omitted pre-discharge Protest with that synthesis so that the founding information object can no longer be processed prospectively without the existence of the contrary patient account being apparent. Taken together, the interventions address the suspected mechanism at its three critical stages: construction, retrospective validation and continuing propagation.

This repository picks up the journey now and moves it toward examination and ethical accountability.
